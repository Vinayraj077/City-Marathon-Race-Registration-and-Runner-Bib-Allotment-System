About the Marathon

The City Marathon is an annual running event that brings thousands of runners together. This website covers race registration and the allotment of runner bib numbers, so every participant is identified, timed and supported on race day.


Date: Sunday, 10 January 2027
Venue / Start: Guru Gobind Singh Stadium
Registration closes: 31 December 2026

Race Rules

Please read these before you register.

  • Minimum age: 18 for Full/Half, 14 for 10K, 10 for 5K.
  • Runners under 18 need consent from a parent or guardian.
  • Bib must be worn on the front of your shirt, fully visible.
  • Bibs are non-transferable to another runner.
  • Follow the marked route and volunteer instructions.
  • Carry a valid photo ID when collecting your race kit.
  • Registration fee is non-refundable.
  • Runners crossing the cut-off time may be asked to stop.
  • Organisers may change the schedule for safety reasons.
