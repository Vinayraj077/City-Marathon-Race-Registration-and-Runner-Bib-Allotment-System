Runner Bib Allotment

How your bib number is assigned and collected

Bib Number Ranges

Each category has its own block of bib numbers, allotted in order of confirmed registration.

Note: Bib numbers are issued by the organising team after your registration and payment are confirmed. Your bib number will be sent to your registered email address.

Bib Allotment Process

What happens after you submit the registration form.

Verification

Your details and age eligibility are checked.

Payment Confirmed

Registration fee is received and recorded.

Bib Allotted

Next free number in your category range is assigned.

Email Sent

You receive your bib number and kit collection details.

Race Kit Collection

  • Kit collection date, venue and timings are shared in your confirmation email.
  • Bring a printed or digital copy of your confirmation email.
  • Carry the same photo ID used in registration.
  • Collect your bib, timing chip, T-shirt and goodies.
  • Kits are not given out on race day.

Wearing Your Bib

  • Pin it flat on the front of your shirt using all four corners.
  • Do not fold, cut or cover the bib.
  • Do not swap or share your bib with another runner.
  • Keep the timing chip attached throughout the race.
