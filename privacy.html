Privacy & Terms

How we handle your information and what you agree to

Information we collect

When you register, the form asks for your name, date of birth, gender, email, mobile number, city, photo ID number, race category, T-shirt size, previous best time, emergency contact, blood group and medical notes.

Why we collect it

  • To verify your identity and age eligibility.
  • To allot your bib number and send you race and kit collection details.
  • To contact your emergency contact and provide medical help if needed on race day.

Who sees it

Your details are used only by the City Marathon organising team and medical staff for this event. Only your bib number, name, category and city appear in the published runner list.

Demo website notice

This website is an HTML and CSS project. The registration form does not store or send the details entered into it.

Race terms

  • You confirm that your details are correct and that you are medically fit to run.
  • The registration fee is non-refundable and bibs are non-transferable.
  • Runners under 18 need consent from a parent or guardian.
  • You must follow the marked route and instructions from volunteers and officials.
  • Runners crossing the cut-off time may be asked to stop.
  • Organisers may change the schedule for safety reasons.

Questions

For any question about your information or these terms, please contact the organisers.
