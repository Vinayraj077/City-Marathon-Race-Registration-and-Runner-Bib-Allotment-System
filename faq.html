Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you register

When and where is the marathon?

The race is on Sunday, 10 January 2027, starting at Guru Gobind Singh Stadium. Reporting and bag drop open at 04:30 AM. See the full schedule on the About page.

When does registration close?

Registration closes on 31 December 2026 . Bibs are allotted in order of confirmed registration, so register early.

Which category should I choose?

Choose the distance that matches your training. The Categories page lists fees, cut-off times and age limits for the Full Marathon, Half Marathon, 10K Run and 5K Fun Run.

What is the minimum age?

18 for the Full and Half Marathon, 14 for the 10K Run and 10 for the 5K Fun Run. Runners under 18 need consent from a parent or guardian.

How is my bib number decided?

Each category has its own block of numbers. After your registration and payment are confirmed, the next free number in your category range is assigned and emailed to you. See Bib Allotment.

Can I transfer my bib to a friend?

No. Bibs are non-transferable, and you must not swap or share your bib with another runner.

Can I get a refund?

The registration fee is non-refundable.

How do I collect my race kit?

Bring your confirmation email and the same photo ID you used to register. Collect your bib, timing chip, T-shirt and goodies. Kits are not given out on race day. Collection date, venue and timings are shared in your confirmation email.

What if I cannot finish within the cut-off time?

Runners crossing the cut-off time may be asked to stop by the organisers. Cut-off times for each category are on the Categories page.

Can the schedule change?

Yes. Organisers may change the schedule for safety reasons. Any change will be communicated to registered runners.

I have a question that is not answered here.

Please use the Contact page. The helpdesk is open 10 AM – 6 PM, Monday to Saturday.
